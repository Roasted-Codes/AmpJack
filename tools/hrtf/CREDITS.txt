What is in this repository
  Only the derived filter coefficients in xbox360/surround_tab.h (5 filters x 32, 64 and
  128 taps, 16-bit), not the measurements. The SOFA file stays outside the repository;
  download it from the link above to regenerate.

What was changed (modified data, Apache 2.0 section 4(b))
  tools/hrtf/gen.py does, in order:
  1. Takes the ear-level measurements at 0, +30, -30, +110 and -110 degrees azimuth.
  2. Diffuse-field equalisation: the power average over all 8802 directions, each weighted
     by its solid angle (spherical Voronoi cell), smoothed to 1/3 octave, inverted, limited
     to +/-12 dB. The set is already diffuse-field equalised, so the correction is within
     +/-0.1 dB.
  3. Assumes a left/right symmetric head: the near-ear response is the power average of
     the left speaker's left ear and the right speaker's right ear; the same for the far ear.
  4. Smooths each magnitude response to 1/6 octave and rebuilds it as minimum phase.
  5. Interaural time difference: read by cross-correlation below 1.5 kHz (front 15.0
     samples = 312 us, surround 35.9 samples = 747 us at 48 kHz) and put back as a
     whole-sample delay on the far ear, less the low-frequency delay the minimum-phase far
     ear already has. In the 32-tap table the surround delay is capped at 24 samples so
     that a quarter of the taps is left for the far ear's response.
  6. Windows each response to the table length (cos^2 fade over the last quarter).
  7. Forms sum and difference filters (near + far, near - far) for the front and surround
     pairs, and one centre filter.
  8. Scales for the console: loudness matched to the plain stereo downmix for front-channel
     pink noise (ITU-R BS.1770 K-weighting; gen.py --headroom lowers it about 4.2 dB
     instead, so that even full-scale white noise on all five channels cannot clip);
     quantised to Q14 with sum|h| <= 2.0 per filter.
