Headphone surround: what to listen to (any headphones, on the Mac; not speakers)

These are made on the Mac by the same maths the console will run (tools/hrtf/demo.py, 64-tap
table). "plain" is what the headset plays today; "surround" is the new mode.

walk-noise-surround.wav   A hiss steps round the room: front-left, centre, front-right,
                          back-right, back-left. Listen for: centre in front of you (not
                          inside your head), and the back two sounding behind and to the
                          side, not just "left" and "right".
walk-noise-plain.wav      The same thing today: front-left and back-left sound identical.
walk-voice-surround.wav   The same walk with a voice-like sound (one vowel per speaker).
scene-plain.wav           A 6-second 5.1 scene as today: plucked notes front-left, ticks
                          front-right, voice centre, rain at the back, a hiss going round
                          the room once (front, right, behind, left, front), a low thump.
scene-surround.wav        The same scene in surround.
scene-switching.wav       The same scene, switching plain / surround every 1.5 s (plain
                          first), with the console's fade at each switch: listen for clicks.

Surround and plain are matched in loudness, so switching should not sound louder or
quieter, only different.
